## D. Article provenance contract

각 질환 article은 공개 전 다음 항목을 고정한다.

```text
definition_source:
symptom_source:
emergency_source:
testing_source:
treatment_overview_source:
prevention_source:
population_specific_source:
retrieved_at:
reviewed_at:
reviewed_by:
review_type: SOURCE_CHECKED | CLINICIAN_REVIEWED
```

`CLINICIAN_REVIEWED`는 실제 검토자 이름·자격·검토일·동의가 있을 때만 허용한다.
