## Independent numerical checks

- Camera scale: `0.424° / 800 = 0.000530°/pixel = 1.908 arcsec/pixel`.
- Full Earth angular diameter from the rounded inputs: `2 atan((12,742/2) / 6,400,000,000) = 1.9909375×10^-6 rad = 0.000114072316° = 0.410660338 arcsec`.
- Full-disk scale in camera pixels: `0.000114072316 / 0.000530 = 0.215230785 pixel`.
- One-way light time from the rounded distance: `6.4×10^12 m / 299,792,458 m/s = 21,348.102 s = 5 h 55 min 48 s`.
- The 0.215-pixel result estimates the full Earth diameter from rounded geometry. NASA's 0.12-pixel record describes the illuminated crescent in the image. They are not presented as measurements of the same image dimension.
