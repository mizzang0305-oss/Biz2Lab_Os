## Independent numerical checks

- `12.0 × 4.448222 = 53.378664 N·s`.
- Missing impulse: `53.378664 - 12.0 = 41.378664 N·s`.
- Correct velocity change for the explicitly synthetic 600kg object: `53.378664 / 600 = 0.08896444 m/s`.
- Misread velocity change: `12.0 / 600 = 0.02 m/s`.
- One-event difference: `0.06896444 m/s`; ten identical, collinear synthetic events: `0.6896444 m/s`.
- The synthetic mass and event count are not Mars Climate Orbiter values and are not used to reconstruct its trajectory.

## Asset and claim limits

- The SVG is a Biz2Lab original diagram. It reproduces no NASA figure, spacecraft artwork or mission data.
- The diagram and table label the example as synthetic and provide the same values as text alternatives.
- The investigation names one root cause and eight contributing causes. The manuscript does not reduce the event to one careless person or claim that conversion alone would have guaranteed success.
- NASA's current mission page summarizes the orbiter as having burned up. Because the Phase I board said it may have been destroyed or may have returned to heliocentric space, the manuscript reports that its exact final state was not directly confirmed.
