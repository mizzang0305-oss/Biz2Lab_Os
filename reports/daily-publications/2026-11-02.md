## Sources actually opened on 2026-11-02

1. E. H. Simpson (1951), original PDF, https://math.bme.hu/~marib/bsmeur/simpson.pdf. Pages 240–241 and tables 2–4 distinguish interpretations of identical table structures. The article's synthetic numbers are not copied from these tables.
2. R datasets documentation, https://search.r-project.org/R/refmans/datasets/html/UCBAdmissions.html. Confirms 4,526 observations from the six largest Berkeley graduate departments in 1973; not the entire university or present-day admissions.
3. Bickel, Hammel and O'Connell (1975), PubMed abstract and bibliographic record, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17835295/. Only the publicly available abstract was read, not the full paywalled paper. The manuscript does not conclude that all forms of discrimination were absent.
